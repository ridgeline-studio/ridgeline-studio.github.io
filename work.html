Featured work

Ancient Forest

The reference piece for the studio: a full environment generated from procedural geometry and shaders.

The scene

Buttress-rooted trees, layered foliage, a shallow stream and an uneven forest floor. The trees and ground cover are emitted from reusable generators; bark grain and soil variation come from procedural shaders rather than image maps.

The look is photoreal with a deliberate faceted edge, so the constructed geometry stays readable instead of resolving into plastic smoothness.

Specifications

  • Engine Cycles / CPU
  • Resolution 1600 × 1000
  • Scene ~5.25 million vertices
  • Assets Built in-house, reusable
  • External art None
