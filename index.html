Studio note

Built, not downloaded.

Fully procedural

Geometry, bark grain, leaf cards and ground cover are generated from code. No third-party models, image textures or HDRIs enter the work.

Detail first

The look is photoreal with a deliberate faceted edge — real light and atmosphere, but with readable constructed forms rather than plastic smoothness.

Reusable by design

Assets are metrically scaled, based at z = 0, organised into named collections and exported with clean UVs for straightforward placement.
